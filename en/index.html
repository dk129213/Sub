Our Story

A small kitchen with the whole sea beside it.

In Srebreno, a quiet bay just east of Dubrovnik, we cook the way our grandmothers did, and the way travelers wished they could.

Our kitchen runs all day: pizza baked to order, tagliatelle in truffle sauce, grilled squid and fish and chips, burgers on brioche buns, ćevapi with warm flat bread.

Breakfast is served until 11:30. The cakes and desserts are made in house.
